TIMELINE

A Journey Through Bhutan

17th Century

Unification

Zhabdrung Ngawang Namgyal established a lasting political and spiritual foundation for Bhutan.

1907

Modern Monarchy

Ugyen Wangchuck became the first hereditary Druk Gyalpo, beginning the Wangchuck dynasty.

1970s

Opening to the World

Bhutan gradually expanded international connections while emphasizing preservation of its culture.

2008

Constitutional Democracy

Bhutan transitioned to a democratic constitutional monarchy and held its first parliamentary elections.
